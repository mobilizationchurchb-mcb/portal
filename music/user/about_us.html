Who we are

About Mobilization Church Balilihan

A church community committed to growing in faith, sharing God's Word, building meaningful relationships, and serving others with love.

Mobilization Church Balilihan values worship, prayer, discipleship, fellowship, and the teaching of Scripture. We encourage every believer to develop a consistent relationship with God and put His Word into practice in everyday life.

This digital platform is one way to make church information and spiritual growth resources more accessible to our members and visitors. It brings devotional materials, ministry schedules, worship music information, and certificate requests together in one convenient place.

Our encouragement: Keep seeking God, remain faithful in the little things, and let your faith be seen through love and service.

First visit

Getting Started

Follow these simple steps when using the church website.

1

Open the Home page

Start from Home to see the latest published information, featured devotional, monthly overview, and shortcuts to other pages.

2

Use the navigation menu

Select Daily Grace, Monthly Schedule, Music Line Up, Certificate, or About Us. On mobile devices, open the navigation menu using its menu button.

3

Choose your preferred theme

Use the Light/Dark control in the navigation. Your selected appearance is shared by supported pages on the same browser.

4

Keep your progress device in mind

Daily completion tracking may be saved in your browser. Use the same device and browser when you want to continue viewing that locally saved progress.

Daily spiritual growth

How to Use the Daily Devotional

Set aside a quiet moment each day to read, reflect, pray, and apply God's Word.

1

Open Daily Grace / Devotional Challenge

Choose the Daily Grace page from the navigation or Home page shortcut. Select the date or devotional entry you want to read.

2

Read the devotional details

Review the devotional title, Scripture reference, Bible verse text, main devotional message, and daily Bible reading assignment.

3

Read the assigned Bible passage

Open your Bible and complete the daily reading assignment. Read the passage in context and take time to understand its message.

4

Reflect and apply the message

Think about what the passage teaches, what it reveals about God, and what action you can take in your personal life today. Pray and put the lesson into practice.

5

Mark the devotional as completed

When you have completed the reading and reflection, use the page's completion control if available. Check the page's confirmation or progress indicator before leaving.

Helpful reminder: A completion mark is a personal progress record, not a replacement for actually reading Scripture and applying it. If progress is stored in your browser, clearing browser data or changing devices may affect what is displayed.
Your journey

Understanding Monthly Progress

Use the monthly overview to see your devotional activity for the selected month.

  • Calendar days: Review the dates that have devotional entries.
  • Completed days: See which daily readings you have marked as completed.
  • Completion percentage: Understand your progress compared with the published devotional days for that month.
  • Remaining days: See how many eligible devotional days are still unfinished.
  • Month selection: If the page provides a month selector, choose another month to review that period.

Progress totals depend on the devotional entries published by the administrators and the completion records available to your browser.

Recognition

How to Generate a Certificate

The certificate page allows eligible participants to submit a request for monthly devotional challenge recognition.

1

Complete the month's devotional challenge

Read and complete all required published devotional days for the month you want to request. Check your monthly progress first.

2

Open Generate Certificate

Choose Certificate / Generate Certificate from the navigation. Select the correct challenge month and year.

3

Review your completion eligibility

Check the displayed total days, completed days, and completion percentage. The request form may only be available when the required completion condition is met.

4

Complete the request form

Enter your full name carefully and provide the requested device type and brand if those fields are shown. Confirm the month and year before submitting.

5

Submit and wait for review

Submit your request once. Keep the request status information available and check the page again later for an administrator's approval or update.

6

Preview and save your certificate

When the request is approved and a certificate ID is issued, review your name and certificate details. Use the available Download PDF, Download PNG, or Print Certificate option.

Important: Submitting a request does not mean it is automatically approved. Certificate availability and issuance depend on the completion rules and administrator review. Contact the church team if your request status appears incorrect.
Plan and participate

How to Use the Monthly Schedule

The Monthly Schedule page helps you see published activities and identify what is happening on a particular date.

1

Open Monthly Schedule

Choose Monthly Schedule from the navigation. The calendar displays the currently selected month.

2

Move between months

Use the previous and next month arrows to browse. Use Today to return to the current date, if available.

3

Choose a calendar date

Select a date to view its published schedule details in the agenda panel. Activity markers help distinguish devotional entries, church activities, and worship music.

4

Filter or search activities

Use the activity filter to narrow the calendar and the search field to find a title or related schedule detail.

5

Print when needed

Use Print Schedule if you need a paper copy for personal reference or ministry coordination.

Schedules may be updated by church administrators. Check the page again before an activity in case details have changed.
Worship ministry

How to View the Worship Music Line Up

Use the Music Line Up page to prepare for worship services and stay informed about the songs selected by the worship team.

  1. Open Music Line Up from the navigation menu.
  2. Browse the available service dates or monthly listings.
  3. Review the worship leader and the published song list.
  4. Check the song keys or other music notes when provided.
  5. Use the information to prepare and practice before the scheduled service.

For worship team members: Follow the latest instructions from your worship leader or music director. If a song, key, or service detail is missing or appears outdated, confirm it with the team rather than assuming the previous version is final.

Responsible use

Account, Device & Website Safety

A few good practices help protect your information and keep the platform working smoothly.

  • Use the official church website link shared by the church.
  • Do not share administrator login credentials with other people.
  • Never enter your personal password into a page unless you are sure it is the official sign-in page.
  • Keep your browser updated and avoid using shared public devices for sensitive activities.
  • If you use a shared device, close the browser session when finished.
  • Do not submit another person's personal information without their permission.
  • Report suspicious links, unexpected messages, or incorrect public information to the church administrators.
Your information matters

Privacy & Data Information

This section explains general information about how to use the platform responsibly. It is not a substitute for a formal privacy notice approved by the church.

Information you may provide. Depending on the feature, the platform may process information such as a name entered for a certificate request, challenge month and year, device details requested by the form, and information submitted through website features.

Website content and database. Published devotional materials, schedules, music lineups, and certificate request information may be stored or retrieved through the church's Supabase database. Access depends on the permissions and security policies configured for the website.

Browser storage. Some preferences and devotional completion records may be stored locally in your browser. Local browser data may not automatically transfer to another phone, computer, or browser. Clearing site data may remove locally stored preferences or progress.

Purpose of use. Information should be used to provide church website features, display ministry information, track challenge progress where supported, and process certificate requests.

Sharing and access. Do not assume that every item is private simply because it appears on the website. Administrators with authorized database access may be able to manage submitted records. Publicly displayed information should not contain sensitive personal details.

Security. The church should maintain appropriate database access controls, secure administrator accounts, and review public access policies. Never publish secret API keys or service-role credentials in browser code.

Your choices. Avoid submitting unnecessary personal information. For questions about a record or a request to correct information, contact the church through its official communication channel.

Privacy reminder: This page describes expected platform behavior based on the features provided. The church should review and publish its official privacy policy, retention period, responsible contact person, and any applicable consent process before treating this text as its complete legal privacy notice.
Need help?

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about the church platform.

Do I need to create an account to read devotionals?

Follow the access instructions shown on the live website. Public devotional pages may be viewable without an account; if a page asks you to sign in, use only the official church sign-in process.

Why is my devotional completion not showing?

Check that you marked the correct date as completed and that you are using the same browser and device. Some progress records are stored locally and may not synchronize between devices.

Can I request a certificate before completing the month?

The certificate request feature is intended for participants who meet the published monthly completion requirement. Complete the required devotional days and check your eligibility on the certificate page.

Why is my certificate request still pending?

Requests may require administrator review. Please allow time for processing and contact the church if the status remains pending longer than expected.

Can I download my certificate immediately after submitting?

Usually, certificate download options become available only after approval and issuance of a certificate ID. Follow the status shown on the certificate page.

Why can I not see a schedule or music lineup?

The administrator may not have published entries for that date or month yet. Refresh the page and contact the ministry team if you believe information is missing.

Does Light/Dark Mode change my account or devotional progress?

No. The theme changes the page appearance only. It should not change your devotional records or certificate request information.

Will my progress move to a new phone?

Not necessarily. If completion is saved only in your browser's local storage, it remains on that browser and device unless the application provides a separate account-based synchronization feature.

What should I do if I entered the wrong name on a certificate request?

Contact an authorized church administrator and provide the relevant request details. Do not submit repeated requests unless the administrator instructs you to do so.

Who updates devotionals, schedules, and music lists?

Authorized church administrators or ministry team members publish and update the information through the church's management process.
