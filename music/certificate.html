The Daily Grace Certificate Challenge is a special program of Mobilization Church Balilihan that encourages every believer to build a consistent habit of daily devotion. To earn your certificate, you simply need to complete the required number of devotional days each month. Every devotion you record in the Daily Grace Devotional page is automatically counted — the more days you complete, the closer you get to your goal. This challenge is not about perfection, but about growing in faithfulness and letting God's Word dwell in you richly.

Your progress is tracked in real time. The system counts how many days you have finished for the current month and compares it to the monthly requirement. Once you reach the target, you will be eligible to generate your official certificate — complete with your name, the month completed, and a seal of recognition. The full certificate generation feature is coming soon and is targeted for release in March. Until then, keep adding your daily devotionals and watch your progress grow!
